## 4. Origins of the Indonesian language

The Indonesian language developed from other languages spoken in Indonesia, for example Malay.

## 5. The year 1928

Indonesian was declared the language of unity by the young people of that era in 1928.

## 6. Indonesian on the international stage

Today, Indonesian is starting to gain international recognition.

## 7. Formal language and the KBBI

When using Indonesian, it is better to use formal language that follows the KBBI (the official Indonesian dictionary). Choose language that is easy for the listener to understand, and articulation should also be clear.

## 8. Example: "karna" vs "karena"

Take the word "karna". If we translate it into another language, its meaning could change. The correct form is **"karena"**.
