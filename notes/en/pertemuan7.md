# Meeting 7: Spelling and Punctuation

These notes are from the lecturer's explanation in the seventh lecturemeeting. Matters concerning EYD fall under the Minister of Education.

## 1. Opening Questions

**1. What do you know about EYD?**

EYD (*Ejaan yang Disempurnakan*, "Perfected Spelling") is the Indonesian spelling guideline that governs the use of letters, the writing of words, the use of punctuation, and the writing of loan elements. EYD was made official in 1972 as an improvement on the previous spelling system.

**2. When were the first and the latest EYD established and published?**

- **First EYD:** established on August 16, 1972, under Presidential Decree No. 57 of 1972. Afterward, the Department of Education and Culture distributed a booklet titled *Pedoman Ejaan Bahasa Indonesia yang Disempurnakan* (Guidelines for the Perfected Indonesian Spelling).
- **Latest EYD (Fifth Edition):** established on August 16, 2022, through Decree of the Head of the Language Development and Fostering Agency No. 0424/I/BS.00.01/2022, then launched or announced on August 18, 2022.

## 2. Definition of Spelling

Spelling is the representation of language sounds (letters, phonemes, words, sentences, and so on) through standardized, meaningful writing (letter) conventions.

## 3. Definition of Punctuation

Punctuation marks are symbols that do not correspond to phonemes (sounds), words, or phrases; instead, they serve to indicate the structure and organization of a piece of writing, as well as the intonation and pauses that can be observed when it is read aloud.

## 4. Types of Spelling Systems

### 4.1 Van Ophuijsen Spelling (1901–1947)

This spelling system was a development of Malay spelling using the Latin alphabet, created by Prof. Charles van Ophuijsen with the assistance of Engku Nawawi, titled Sutan Makmur, and Muhammad Taib Sutan Ibrahim.

| Current form | Van Ophuijsen Spelling |
| --- | --- |
| yakni | ja'ni |
| payung | payoeng |

### 4.2 Soewandi Spelling (1947–1972)

The Soewandi Spelling was a Latin-alphabet spelling system for the Indonesian language. It was set out in Decree of the Minister of Education and Culture Mr. Soewandi No. 264 Bhg. A, dated March 19, 1947.

| Current form | Soewandi Spelling |
| --- | --- |
| khusus | chusus |
| Jumat | djumat |
| yakni | jakni |

### 4.4 Malindo Spelling (1959)

The Malindo Spelling was the result of formulating a joint Malay and Indonesian spelling. This formulation took place at the 1954 Indonesian Language Congress in Medan, North Sumatra. The system was never put into everyday use because of the confrontation between Indonesia and Malaysia at the time, and it was not published due to political problems between the two countries.

### 4.5 LBK Spelling (1967)

The Institute of Language and Literature (LBK), which was later renamed the National Language Institute, the Language Center, the Center for Language Development and Fostering, and finally the Language Development and Fostering Agency (BPPB), issued a new spelling system as a development of the Malindo Spelling, whose status had remained uncertain. This system already included many improvements, so there was almost no difference between it and EYD.

> **Note:** The Renewal Spelling (1957), the Malindo Spelling (1959), and the LBK Spelling (1967) were never published.

### 4.6 EYD (1972)

On August 16, 1972, the President of the Republic of Indonesia made EYD official under Presidential Decree No. 57 of 1972. Afterward, the Department of Education and Culture distributed a booklet titled *Pedoman Ejaan Bahasa Indonesia yang Disempurnakan* (Guidelines for the Perfected Indonesian Spelling), which became the standard reference for spelling usage.

### 4.7 EBI or PUEBI (2015)

PUEBI stands for *Pedoman Umum Ejaan Bahasa Indonesia* (General Guidelines for Indonesian Spelling). This spelling system emerged alongside advances in science and technology and was established through Regulation of the Minister of Education and Culture No. 50 of 2015.

PUEBI governs:

- The use of letters.
- The writing of words.
- The use of punctuation.
- The writing of loan elements.

There are **7 differences** between EYD and EBI, namely in:

- The writing of the diphthong *ei*.
- The use of bold type.
- The use of capital letters.
- The use of punctuation, including:
  - The ellipsis.
  - The comma.
  - Numbers and the hyphen.
  - Parentheses.

### 4.8 EYD Fifth Edition (2022)

The Fifth Edition of EYD was established on August 16, 2022, through Decree of the Head of the Language Development and Fostering Agency No. 0424/I/BS.00.01/2022. This guideline brought back the name EYD and replaced PUEBI.
